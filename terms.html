Public Beta Terms

Draft for beta review: these are product-boundary terms, not a substitute for legal counsel. Review before broad public launch.

Beta service

4EVER FORWARD is an early-stage navigation and continuity tool. Features, data structures, resources, and availability can change during the beta.

No emergency dispatch

The service is not an emergency service and does not dispatch responders. In an emergency, use the appropriate local emergency service. U.S. users can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and 911 for immediate emergency response.

No professional advice

The service does not replace medical, legal, financial, or other licensed professional advice. Resource and eligibility information should be confirmed with the official provider.

Your account

Keep your account credentials private. Do not use the beta to store passwords for other services or highly sensitive identifiers.

User control

You decide whether to synchronize your browser journey, what optional information to enter, and what prepared snapshots to share. The beta may retain limited security and audit events needed to operate the service.
