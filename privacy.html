Public Beta Privacy & Data Notice

Draft for beta review: this notice describes the current product design and should receive formal legal/privacy review before a broad production launch.

What the beta stores

Account email, encrypted journey-state snapshots for the explicitly approved 4EVER FORWARD data namespaces, synchronization metadata, and limited security/audit events.

What we ask you not to enter

Do not enter Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, financial account credentials, medical records, precise location, passwords for other services, or similarly sensitive identifiers into free-text fields.

How journey data is protected

Cloud journey snapshots are encrypted before database storage. Account passwords are one-way derived rather than stored directly. Session cookies are HttpOnly and production deployments require HTTPS.

Your control

Cloud sync begins only after you create an account, verify your email, and explicitly choose whether to upload the current device or restore the server copy. Account controls include data export and server-side account deletion.

Sharing

This public beta does not give providers automatic access to your full journey. Features that prepare a shareable snapshot are designed to let the user choose what to share and with whom.

Emergency boundary

4EVER FORWARD does not continuously monitor you, track your location, or dispatch emergency services.

Questions about this beta should be directed to the support contact published by the operator before launch.
